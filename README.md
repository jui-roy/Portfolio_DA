I turn messy data into decisions people act on. This repo holds my portfolio website, two Excel workbooks and a PDF of my case studies.



## Projects

**1. Superstore: where does the business lose money?** (Python, SQL)
Order lines discounted over 40% run at about -77% margin, while undiscounted lines earn 29.5%.

**2. Telecom churn: who leaves, and what does it cost?** (Python, SQL)
Month-to-month customers churn at 42.7% versus 2.8% on two-year contracts, costing about $139K a month.

**3. HR attrition dashboard** (Excel)
Overtime workers leave at 30.5% versus 10.4%. Sales Representatives leave at 39.8%.

**4. Netflix catalogue analysis** (Excel)
Titles added per year rose from 90 in 2015 to 2,349 in 2019. Data cleaned with formulas only.
